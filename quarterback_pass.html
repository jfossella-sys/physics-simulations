Challenges

  1. With the default settings, find the receiver’s position at the moment of release. Then calculate the throw angle that completes the pass. Test your answer.
  2. Set the running direction to 0° (a straight “go” route). How does the required throw angle change if the QB releases the ball 1 s later?
  3. Slow the ball down to 9 m/s and make the receiver run directly away at 10 m/s. Can the pass ever be completed? Explain using relative velocity.
  4. Turn on vector components. Show that the ball’s x- and y-motions are independent and each has constant velocity.

Top-down view: x points downfield, y points across the field, and angles are measured counterclockwise from +x. Air resistance and the ball’s vertical arc are ignored. Shortcuts: Space snap or pause, R reset.

Try this

  1. Set the current to 0. Where does the boat land?
  2. Double the current speed. What happens to the crossing time? What happens to the drift?
  3. Double the boat speed instead. How do the time and drift change now?
  4. Launch the boat and watch which way it points. Is its heading the same as its path?
  5. Predict the drift for a 150 m river, a 3 m/s boat and a 2 m/s current. Then check it.

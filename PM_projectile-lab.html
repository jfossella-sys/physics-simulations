Projectile Lab

Pick a launch style, drag the sliders, then hit Launch. Turn on air resistance to see how the real world bends the rules.

Results

Launch style


Drag force = 0.5 x air density x Cd x area x speed squared. Bigger and lighter balls feel it more.


Range at every angle (0 to 90)

With no air on level ground, 25 and 65 degrees (or 30 and 60) land in the same spot, and 45 goes farthest. Add air or a start height and the best angle shifts below 45.

Pick an object

Choosing one turns on air resistance and sets mass, size and drag. Light and fluffy things get slowed down the most.
